Pokéthology

Features & Sections

Everything inside Pokéthology

Gemini AI

🧠 Pokéthology Section

The beating heart of the project. Features a personal chatbot called Pokéthology AI using Google's Gemini, specially trained with Pokémon knowledge and FAQs to ensure deep insights for fans and curious trainers alike.

1v1 Simulator

⚔️ Combat Section

A dynamic and fast 1v1 battle simulator with options for 3D models or Original Sprites. Includes random setups and a "Chaos Mode" for unpredictable surprise matchups tied into daily arena missions.

Full Database

📊 Stats Section

Detailed analysis for every single Pokémon, including Mega and Gigantamax forms. Inspect moves, types, base stats, and original game descriptions, with a dedicated tool to compare two Pokémon side-by-side.

Missions

🎯 Daily Hub Section

The control center for fresh daily objectives. Tackles unique Pokémon Theory questions and high-stakes battle scenarios. Reaching Master rank will test your dedication to the limit!

Trivia Challenge

🎓 Theory Exam Section

A true intellectual challenge spanning across all Pokémon regions. Tackle 3 unique questions per region each day—a total of 27 questions to prove your mastery.

Daily Discovery

🔍 Daily Scan Section

Randomly spotlights and scans a Pokémon each day from the entire roster. Discover new species, analyze their attributes, and jump straight into battle if inspiration strikes.

Master Ball Challenge

🏆 Pokéthology Mission Section

Win at least one arena battle with every type and every single Pokémon in the Pokédex, including alternative forms. Resets monthly—aim for the top Master Ball rank!

Profile & Teams

⭐ Avatar & Favorites Section

Equip classic protagonist or antagonist avatars with original sprites and lore descriptions. Save your favorite team members with quick-access shortcuts straight into stats or battle arena.

Original Soundtrack

🎧 Mikel's Music

Endlessly grateful to Mikel for featuring his famous "Poké & Chill" playlist as the official soundtrack. Manage audio directly in the app settings: pick individual tracks, loop, or shuffle the full playlist while exploring.
